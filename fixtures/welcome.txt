YOUR WORLD, WITHIN REACH

This is a small, original sample for Obelisk's demo library.

Obelisk keeps files in ordinary folders. Each item has an item.json manifest
containing its title, original source, local path, acquisition date, and SHA-256.
The database and the HTML library can be regenerated from those manifests.

Try importing a document, then open START_HERE.html directly from the archive.
Copy the whole archive folder to preserve relative links.

A recorded checksum is a baseline for later integrity checks. It does not prove
that a file is authentic, safe to run, or readable on every operating system.
